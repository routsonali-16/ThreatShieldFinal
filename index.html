RESPONSE PLAYBOOK

Action Center

Turn a suspicious result into concrete next steps.

01

Do not pay upfront

Pause when an unsolicited loan offer asks for a processing fee before a verified lending relationship is established.

02

Verify independently

Open the bank/NBFC's official website yourself and verify the DLA/LSP relationship. Do not rely on a link supplied by the sender.

03

Protect evidence

Save screenshots, URLs, phone numbers, payment receipts and the exact message before blocking or reporting.

04

Check your credit report

Look for unfamiliar enquiries or accounts. Use the bureau's official dispute route if something does not belong to you.

05

Report suspicious activity

Use official reporting channels such as RBI guidance and the appropriate cybercrime/law-enforcement route.

06

Never share OTPs

ThreatShield will never ask for OTP, PIN, CVV, passwords or full banking credentials.

Important design principle

“RBI-listed” is not an endorsement. The RBI public repository is intended to help verify a DLA's association with a regulated entity; it is based on data submitted by regulated entities. ThreatShield therefore shows “verified association” separately from “risk-free”.
